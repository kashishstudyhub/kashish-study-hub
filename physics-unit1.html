⚛️ Unit 1: Wave Nature of Particles & Schrödinger Equation

Quantum Mechanics

Quantum mechanics is the branch of physics that describes the behaviour of matter and radiation at atomic and microscopic scales.

  • Wave-particle duality
  • Quantization of energy
  • Probability interpretation
  • Schrödinger equation

de Broglie Hypothesis

According to de Broglie, a moving particle has an associated matter wave.

λ = h / p

Where h is Planck's constant and p is the momentum of the particle.

Quantum Operators

An operator is a mathematical operation that acts on a wavefunction to obtain a physical quantity.

Momentum operator: p̂ = -iℏ ∂/∂x

Energy operator: Ê = iℏ ∂/∂t

Time-Dependent Schrödinger Equation

iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ

Here ψ represents the wavefunction and Ĥ is the Hamiltonian operator.

This equation describes how a quantum state changes with time.

Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation

Ĥψ = Eψ

For a particle in a potential V(x), the one-dimensional form is:

−(ℏ²/2m)(d²ψ/dx²) + Vψ = Eψ

Particle in a 1D Box

A particle is confined between two impenetrable walls separated by a distance L.

The allowed energy levels are quantized.

Eₙ = n²h² / 8mL²

where n = 1, 2, 3, ... is the quantum number.

Born Interpretation

The wavefunction itself is not directly observable. The quantity |ψ|² represents the probability density of finding a particle at a particular position.

Probability Density = |ψ|²

The wavefunction must satisfy suitable normalization conditions.

Free Particle

For a free particle, the potential energy is taken as constant, often V = 0.

ψ(x,t) = A e^{i(kx − ωt)}

where k is the wave number and ω is the angular frequency.

Wave Packet

A wave packet is a localized group of waves formed by superposition of waves having different wave numbers and frequencies.

Wave packets are useful for describing localized particles.

Phase Velocity

vₚ = ω / k

Group Velocity

vᵍ = dω / dk

For matter waves, group velocity is associated with the velocity of the particle.

Uncertainty Principle

It is impossible to simultaneously determine the exact position and exact momentum of a particle.

Δx Δp ≥ ℏ/2

This is a fundamental principle of quantum mechanics.

Important Formulas

de Broglie wavelength: λ = h/p

Particle in box: Eₙ = n²h²/8mL²

Phase velocity: vₚ = ω/k

Group velocity: vᵍ = dω/dk

Uncertainty: ΔxΔp ≥ ℏ/2
